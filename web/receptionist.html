A medical receptionist that checks who is calling before it shares anything

Practices lose patients to hold queues and risk privacy mistakes when staff answer in a hurry. This receptionist verifies the caller first, books, cancels and moves visits, and says "booked" only after the record confirms it.

Built for community health centers and specialty practices. Records on this page are simulated and every patient is synthetic.

Safeguards that are built

  • Verification first: name and date of birth, ZIP when two records match.
  • A caller who is not the patient gets no patient details.
  • One change per caller turn. Only offered slots or the caller's own visits.
  • Urgent words route to 911 or 988 guidance, not booking.
  • A write that times out is pending, then checked by reading the record. It is never resent on a guess.
  • An output guard blocks "booked", "cancelled" or "moved" unless confirmed, and blocks details to unverified callers.
  • Call logs are redacted. The audit log is hash-chained and uses pseudonyms.
  • Config and prompts are versioned releases with an eval gate and rollback.

Where the records come from

  • This page uses a simulated practice so answers stay fast.
  • A real adapter for the athenahealth sandbox is built and tested. Sandbox writes were measured at 60 to 150 seconds or more, so proof calls with it are run separately.
  • Reads are retried. Writes are never retried blindly.

What we do not claim

  • No HIPAA compliance or certification claim. The design follows HIPAA-minded habits only.
  • No real patients and no production calls. Every record is synthetic.
  • Business associate agreements with every vendor are not in place.
  • Encryption at rest, intake forms and text messages are not built.
  • Live latency and cost for this agent are not measured yet.
  • Sandbox cancel and reschedule behavior is only partly tested.
