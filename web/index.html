A clinic phone agent that says "booked" only when the record says so

Clinics lose callers to dead air and wrong bookings. This agent answers inbound calls, books every family member exactly once, and tells the caller "booked" only after the clinic record confirms it.

Built for community health centers and specialty practices. All patient data here is synthetic.

    Safeguards that are built

    • Bookings are idempotent: one attempt, one appointment, no blind retries.
    • The agent hangs up only after the caller says goodbye and no work is pending.
    • A silent caller is reprompted twice, then the call closes.
    • Config and prompts live in the repo as versioned releases with an eval gate and a one-command rollback.
    • Call events and cost per turn are logged. The log stores events, not what was said.

    What we do not claim

    • These are test calls by the builder and simulated callers. They are not production calls.
    • No real patients. Every record is synthetic. No HIPAA or other compliance claim.
    • Human transfer to a live person is built but not proven on a live line.
    • Phone stability over many calls is not proven. Some phone calls dropped.
    • The 1.5s reply target is not met. The working target is 2.0s.
    • Overlapping speech on real audio is not measured.
    • The spelling fallback for a misheard name is tested offline, not on a real call.
    • Voice and LiveKit costs are not measured yet.
